## Adoption stages

### Stage 0 — Observe

Run the Tribunal without blocking release. Measure defects caught, false blockers, cost, latency, and repair acceptance.

### Stage 1 — Advise

Require a dossier for high-impact surfaces, but keep release authority fully human.

### Stage 2 — Gate

Allow deterministic blockers and explicit security/accessibility failures to block automatically. Subjective or conflicting findings remain human-required.

### Stage 3 — Compound

Turn accepted findins into design-system rules, fixtures, templates, and regression tests. Do not make every one-off preference canonical.

## Product/service candidates

- **Quirk Design Tribunal Audit** — diagnose a generated product or design system.
- **Cross-Surface Consistency Dossier** — app, docs, email, deck, and campaign reconciliation.
- **One-of-One Concept Duel** — blind comparison for signature launches.
- **Agent-Generated UI Release Gate** — white-label review pack for AI software teams.
- **Template Admission Pack** — turn a good one-off deliverable into a reusable governed template.

Each remains a candidate until cost, repeatability, acceptance, and differentiation are evidenced.
