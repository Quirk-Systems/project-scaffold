# Quality-Bar Grammar

A useful criterion has five fields:

1. **Outcome** — what must be true for the user or system.
2. **Condition** — the context in which it must remain true.
3. **Evidence** — what can prove or disprove it.
4. **Authority** — who may waive or change it.
5. **Release effect** — whether failure blocks release.

## Good

> At 320px, 768px, and 1440px widths, the primary action remains visible without horizontal scrolling. Evidence: three rendered captures plus DOM overflow check. Failure blocks release.

## Bad

> Make it premium and clean.

Translate taste words into observable discriminators. Preserve the original taste word as intent, but do not pretend it is a test.

## Three layers

- **Must** — deterministic, safety, accessibility, integrity, contract compliance.
- **Should** — comparative quality, usability, hierarchy, coherence.
- **Signature** — Quirk distinctiveness, emotional precision, ownability, and avoidance of generic generated aesthetics.

A `signature` criterion can be subjective without becoming vague. Require references, contrasts, and an explanation of what makes the choice specific rather than merely unusual.
